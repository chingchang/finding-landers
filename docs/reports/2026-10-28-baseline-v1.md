## What the numbers say

- **The lists are long because the floor is low on purpose.** 784 per km2 is the whole tail; the key needs
  only the top 50 within 1.5 km of each piece, and at the 2 per km2 operating point the lists are 1873 long.
- **The ground differs from the fitting ground.** Calibration v0 was fitted on viking1, curiosity, spirit
  and pathfinder tiles. Schiaparelli and the second Phoenix strip produce two to three times more
  dark compacts per km2 than the calibration expects, which means their calibrated scores are too generous
  there. A calibration fitted per strip, or on more sites, is the fix; it waits for the key so it can be
  checked against real pieces rather than only against false alarms.
- **Rates per strip are a property of the ground, not of the hardware**: 138 per km2 at Pathfinder
  (50 cm/px, few small components) against 2349 on the shorter Schiaparelli strip.
