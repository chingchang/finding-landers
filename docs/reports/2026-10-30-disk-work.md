## Two strips show no ground texture

The other 7 failed pairs all involve PSP_004336_1035 or PSP_004402_1030. The retry's median correlation on them is
0.016 to 0.051 at both 2 and 4 m per pixel, and no spot passed the acceptance rule (`retry_summary.json`). This looks

What follows from this:

- **Coverage:** the 206.71 km2 of these two strips (`survivors.json`, `per_strip`) count as searched in item 11's map,

- **Injection board:** 12 of the 46 tiles and 35 of the 108 planted pieces sit on these two strips, and 15 of the 35

  Changing the board is your call. I changed nothing.

## What I decided and why

- **Crops are 320 px.** The vote crop is 256 px and its shifts reach 32 px, so 320 keeps votes exact. 256 px would save
  disk, but shifted crops would then contain blank margins.
- **46 tiles, not 54.** `out/transplant_t1` draws 54 tiles, but only 46 received a placement, and those 46 are what your
  decision of 10-29 02:00 names as the tuning ground. I did not add tiles from the search area.
- **Blind sites are refused in code.** Neither of their strips is on disk, and no label of theirs was copied.
- **Sources for the tuning lists:** `out/shape_v3` with `landers/shape_v3.json` applied, as item 9c did, and
  `out/heldout_v1` for the four moved sites. Each strip's `index.json` records its source.
- **New code stays outside the frozen files.** `landers/persist.py` is untouched, and the export also keeps each strip's
  overview, which the plan did not list, because persistence needs it after the delete.
- **The 0.5 m retry uses the same 2 m match radius and node reach as item 11, on the ground.** The node acceptance rule
  is unchanged too.
- **Code state.** The retry ran before the first commit of this item, so `persist_12c/` records a dirty tree; its code
  is what 7dc5708 holds. The export ran at 7dc5708. The tile and label steps came after small edits to
  `scripts/search_export.py` only, and `check.json` records the clean commit the check ran from.
