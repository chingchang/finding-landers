# Item 13: the learned re-ranker does not meet the rule and is not adopted

The rule needed three things, and the re-ranker met one. It beats frozen-v1 on the key, loses on the transplant board,
and pushes two recorded pieces out of the top 20. Nothing changes in the detector or the pipeline. The protocol was
committed before any run (`docs/proposals/reranker-v1-protocol.md`, commit 6b686ef).

Two things need your decision; they are in `docs/proposals/reranker-v1.md`:

1. Whether item 14 runs on frozen-v1's list or on a rank fusion I tried after seeing this result.
2. Whether the two strips with no ground texture stay on the transplant board.

All numbers are from `docs/reports/2026-10-30-reranker/summary.json`, a copy of `out/rerank/summary.json`. Everything
is on tuning sites, and all four sites sealed for frozen-v1 (perseverance, insight, zhurong, beagle2) are **not blind**
here.

Reading these numbers:

- **Spread over the ten sites at N = 20.** Key: mean 0.308, sd 0.338 for frozen-v1; mean 0.515, sd 0.275 for the

- **The transplant comparison at N = 20 rests on 3 allowed false alarms.** It is a noisy number, but it is the one the

## Is the rise on the key real?

The checks below found no leak, but they do not settle it. All of these sites are ones the person has already worked
through, and the key's pieces are few.

- **Shuffled labels.** With the positives drawn at random, 20 runs reach 0 to 2 of 32 at N = 20, median 1. So the rise
  does not come from the folds leaking the labels.
- **Frozen ranks reproduce the person's score.** The perseverance parachute, backshell and rover come out at 25, 44 and
  81, the ranks in the Decisions of 10-29 21:20.
- **Repeat.** The pinned encoder reproduces the first 100 saved crops exactly. All five variants, rerun from a clean
  commit (25a0af2), gave the same counts as the first run.
- **What moved.** Beagle 2's lander on its one listed strip 334 to 1, perseverance rover 81 to 1, schiaparelli parachute
  765 to 5, zhurong backshell 1,824 to 12 (`pieces` in the summary). A person should look at those crops and at each
  re-ranked top 20 before believing this. I did not look at them.

## Is it learning the paste?

Not by the protocol's test. Two cautions:

- **Shams score above ordinary false alarms.** They do so on 8 of 10 sites (median AUC 0.691), even though shams are
  negatives. Shams are plain ground from another strip, pasted in, so some of the paste may still be visible to the
  model. Real copies beat shams at median AUC 0.924, but only about 0.6 on phoenix (0.597) and beagle2 (0.604).
- **Without any shams or copies in training** (the no-transplanted variant), natives score far above copies, with a
  median difference of -2.949. That is the model not recognising planted pieces, which is a gap between native and
  planted crops in the other direction. The two boards may not be measuring the same thing.

## The hackathon requirement

DINOv2 does not count, as far as the sources I could read go, so I would not rely on it. SAM 3.1, Muse Wonder and Muse
Spark carry the requirement.

- The event guide, in a repo that describes itself as a team's copy and not an official document: "Every project must
  use Meta's models:", then "Meta Model API: Muse Spark series, Muse Wonder, Muse Image, Muse Voice Transcribe, SAM 3.1"
  and "Open weights (own hardware or partner inference): Muse Glimmer, Muse Spark Open". DINOv2 and Perception Encoder
  are not listed.
- The official event page says "run Meta open weights models" and does not list which. It said the official rules would
  be published before the event; I did not find them.

The README lists only what the pipeline calls, and DINOv2 is not in the pipeline, so I did not change it. Please check
the official rules if the re-ranker comes back in any form. CLAUDE.md also names the open-weight models it allows, and
DINOv2 is not among them; its use here rests on item 13 naming it.

## What I decided and why

- **A new transplant run of 128 plantings and 128 shams.** The old boards hold 18 pieces and the key holds 32. So the
  frozen-v1 numbers here are not the earlier board's numbers.
- **Tile false alarms as negatives.** The plan lists tuning-list false alarms and shams. I added these because every
  copy sits on polar ground, so the model could otherwise learn that polar ground means hardware. They come from the 46
  listed tiles only, and each detection is scored by a model that left out its own target strip.
- **Logistic regression written with scipy,** so only the embedding pass needs torch and the tests stay light.
- **Strips with no recorded piece were not embedded,** and give no negatives, because their hardware is there but
  unmarked. Of the 14 tuning strips, 12 were used.
- **Off-list pieces are scored at the list's floor.** A piece outside the top 2,000 trains from the crop at its recorded
  position, with the list's lowest score. Its high native score (zhurong lander 10.464) is on a crop centred by a
  person, so it does not show a deeper list would find it.
- **Tile edges.** A detection near a tile's edge has its missing pixels filled with the median, because reading past
  the listed tiles is not allowed. Native crops rarely have this, so it is a difference between the boards.

## Things that went differently from plan

- `uv lock` was denied here. `uv run` updated `uv.lock` when it resolved the new `rerank` group, and that file is
  committed.
- `out/rerank/t10/run.json` records its commit as dirty, at 6b686ef. I expect that was only `uv.lock`, but I did not
  verify it. The planted tiles were not rerun.
- The embedding pass recorded its revision as null. I read `ed25f3a3…` from the weights cache, wrote it into
  `encoder.json` with a note, pinned it in the code, and checked the repeat above.
- `uv run ruff check` flags long lines in the new files, as it does for `scripts/search_export.py`. Everything else in
  ruff passes.
- Local crops embedded: 32,471 against the cap of 80,000, in 644 s on this Mac (`encoder.json`). No paid call was made.

## What this means for the paused delete

The key result raises the value of what sits below rank 2,000. If you adopt any re-ranker, pieces there need pixels the
delete would remove. This adds to the first point in the disk-work report, and I recommend keeping the JP2 files until
you have settled item 14's list.
