# Item 15: per-strip calibration with an honest tail does not meet the rule and is not adopted

The rule needed three things, and the design met two. It beats frozen-v1 on the key and on the transplant board, but two
recorded pieces leave the top 20. Moving the split by half a block also removes both wins, so they are not robust. The
tail itself came out honest: a score now means the same false-alarm rate on ground it was not fitted on. Nothing changes
in the detector or the pipeline. The protocol was committed before any fit (`docs/proposals/halfcal-v1-protocol.md`,
commit 8ae5365).

One thing needs your decision, in `docs/proposals/halfcal-v1.md`: whether v2's list carries the measured rate as a
column. It would change no order.

All numbers are from `docs/reports/2026-10-30-halfcal/summary.json`, a copy of `out/halfcal/results.json`, run at clean
commit 565a608. A first run at 222cc7a gave the same file apart from the commit and the timing. Everything is on tuning sites, and all four sites sealed for frozen-v1 (perseverance, insight, zhurong,
beagle2) are **not blind** here. No paid call was made. Frozen-v1 reproduces item 13's numbers on both boards at every N;
the script stops if it does not.

- **The wins do not survive the split.** The same design with the checkerboard moved 500 m reads key 8 and transplant 14

- **Halves are the only column measured on ground the curve did not see,** and they hold within about 1.4 at levels 0 and
  1 on every tuning strip. A level at or above a strip's cap is not counted, so high levels have fewer strips.
- **A reading near 2 close to the cap comes from the construction.** The cap sits at half a false alarm per colour, and
  about one candidate per branch per colour exceeds the other colour's top.
- **The frozen-v1 table spans more than three orders of magnitude across strips.** On target strips its median is 0 at
  level 1, so the polar ground is far under the rate the table implies.
- **This corrects my reading in `docs/reports/2026-10-29-tuning-loop-3.md`.** That report said the realised rate above 2
  was 10 to 15 times nominal because the tail falls too fast. It compared all branches together with one branch's
  nominal rate. With five branches the nominal is 0.05, not 0.01. Set properly, item 8's tail reads about 2 at level 2
  here (median 2.034), and at most 4.256.

## What an honest tail costs

**A strip cannot say anything about its own top ten.** Each strip has 6 to 21 candidates at the cap, about one per branch
per colour, as the construction implies (`at_cap_in_strip`). Above the cap the data say only "rarer than one in this
colour's area". On those candidates the order comes from the frozen-v1 table and the rule gains, not from anything fitted
here.

That is the range N = 20 is judged in. Between 1 and 19 of each strip's top 20 sit at the cap (`at_cap_in_primary_top_20`).
So at the top of the list, this design ranks by the same table as frozen-v1, after removing the branch imbalance.

The rule gains carry most of the top: without them the key reads 2 of 32 at N = 20 and the transplant board 14.

- **Item 8's tail clears all three conditions at N = 20,** and I am not adopting or proposing it. The protocol fixed it as

  strips did not flood the list as they did in item 8; at 2 per km2 they go 15 to 19.

## Using the search strips

Fitting a strip on itself needs the 9 target strips' frozen-v1 lists. I read only each candidate's raw score, branch and
block, for those strips and only to build curves. No truth is involved and no candidate outside the 46 listed tiles was
ranked, looked at or reported. The aggregate rates per strip are in the summary. The decision of 10-29 02:00 keeps tuning
inside the 46 tiles, and item 8 fitted strip curves from other tiles the same way, but you may read this differently, so
I am raising it again. Only the rule's answer could pass from those lists to a choice, and that answer was no.

## What I decided and why

- **Rescore the frozen lists, no new detector pass.** The candidates stay frozen-v1's, the comparison is exact, and it
  needs no JP2. So this calibration does not depend on the paused delete.
- **Checkerboard of 1,000 m squares,** because ground changes along a strip and two long halves would compare different
  terrain.
- **Cap instead of slope, table order within the cap.** This keeps the score from claiming a rate nobody measured.

## Things that went differently from plan

- **A fix to the protocol's wording.** It said the detector is rerun on item 13's saved planted tiles. That disagreed with
  the record on the second tile (128 of 128 recorded detections matched, plus one extra sheet), because the saved tiles
  are rounded to whole samples while item 13 detected on the unrounded planting (`experiments/halfcal_diff.py`). I repeat
  the planting from item 13's seed instead. All 46 tiles then reproduce every placement and every detection. The check
  stayed as strict as written.
- **A small finding about item 13.** Its tile embeddings were cut from those rounded tiles, so they differ from what the
  detector saw by under one sample value. I expect this to be immaterial and have not measured it.
- **Nobody has looked at these on the pixels.** I did not open any crop, so neither the rises nor the drops have been
  checked by eye.
